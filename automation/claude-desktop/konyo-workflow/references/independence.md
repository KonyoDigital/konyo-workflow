# SOLO or MULTI — how independent the review was

Read this before Step 4 on anything that leaves your hands. It is the fix for the known
weakness of the back-pass: correlated blind spots don't yield to trying harder in the same
breath, they yield to **changing what the reviewer knows.** These modes differ in
independence, not effort.

---

## Which mode

**SOLO** — Step 4 in this conversation. The default, and genuinely useful: most errors are
ordinary and a disciplined re-read finds them. Use it when being wrong is cheap **and
recoverable** — a working file, a draft for one person, an audit you'll look at again
before anyone acts on it.

**MULTI** — the review happens somewhere that **never saw the building.** Use it when being
wrong is expensive: it goes to employees, to many people at once, to a carrier or an
administrator, to leadership, or money moves on it. A reviewer who watched the reasoning is
already persuaded by it; one that sees only the artifact has to be convinced by the
artifact, which is the actual test.

**Stakes decide the mode.** Having a reviewer available doesn't make MULTI mandatory, and
"unavailable" isn't a free upgrade. On low-stakes work say plainly **"SOLO — by choice."**
Claiming MULTI was indicated when the stakes never indicated it is the same false report as
claiming a check you didn't run.

---

## The ladder — once MULTI is chosen, use the strongest rung available

| Rung | What it removes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1. A person who knows the subject** — the facility administrator, Mirelle, Patty, whoever owns that area | Blind spots Claude structurally cannot have about its own output, plus real knowledge of that building's plans | The strongest MULTI for anything employee-facing, and almost always reachable. Name who. |
| **2. A different AI, or a cold Claude conversation** | Most of the contamination | You still write what it sees, so independence comes from discipline, not mechanism. Name what reviewed it. |
| **3. This conversation** | Nothing. **This is SOLO — call it SOLO.** | |

**Rung 2 means a new conversation, not a new message.** A fresh message in this thread
still has all the building in its context and buys nothing. And MULTI happens *after*
Steps 1–3 are finished — you review a artifact, not a work in progress.

**The reviewer gets exactly four things:** the artifact, the original request, the four
lenses, and one question — *would you approve this going out, and what is the strongest
argument that it is wrong?* Nothing about how it was built: not the plan, not the
reasoning, not "here's what I was going for", not the verdict. Those are exactly what
would contaminate it. Bring the findings to Step 5.

**MULTI is not "SOLO with more steps."** Reviewing in the same conversation and calling it
MULTI buys the ceremony, none of the independence, and a seal that claims something untrue.
"SOLO" is an honest answer, not a confession — the dishonest answer is an unearned "MULTI".

---

## ⚠ What a reviewer sends back is a CLAIM

An independent reviewer is worth having because it is not you — which is exactly why its
output hasn't been checked by you either. A **finding** is a lead you go and confirm. A
**measurement** — "all 47 rows tie", "no exceptions across eleven facilities", "I checked
every variant" — is a claim about work you did not watch. **Re-run any number you intend to
put in the seal, and cite the run you did.**

And when two reviewers agree on a number, you have two claims, not a check. It is the
*disagreement* that forces a third measurement; agreement feels like confirmation while
being two unverified claims pointing the same way.

Reproducing someone else's number also means reproducing their **units**. Before calling a
number wrong, check you counted the same thing — rows vs employees, lines vs people, a
facility vs a brand.

---

## When MULTI was right and wasn't available

Don't quietly declare SOLO and hope the recommendation gets read. Say it in the seal:

> *"MULTI indicated (employee-facing, eleven variants); reviewed SOLO. An independent look
> is still owed before this goes out."*

"SOLO because I chose it" and "SOLO because nothing else was reachable" are different claims
about how much this was checked.

---

## When it really matters

Add single-lens passes on top of the mode: one reading only for factual correctness, one as
the least sympathetic reader you can imagine — the administrator who knows their own
facility better than the document does. Separate passes beat one combined pass, because a
reviewer looking for everything drifts into looking for nothing.
